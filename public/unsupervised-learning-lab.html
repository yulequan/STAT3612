Classroom question · How does a bridge affect the three linkages?

Compare cuts that yield the same number of clusters. A succession of short connections can join distant groups under single linkage. Complete linkage considers the farthest cross-cluster pair. Average linkage considers every cross-cluster pair equally.

Classroom question · How many neighbors should the graph use?

Try k = 1, 4, and 24 for the same pair. Isomap constructs a symmetrized k-nearest-neighbor graph, computes shortest-path distances, then applies classical multidimensional scaling to those distances. A small graph can be disconnected. A large neighborhood can introduce edges across the folds, so graph distance can become too short.

Classroom question · Which observations stay close when perplexity or seed changes?

t-SNE matches pairwise similarities in the original space to similarities under a heavy-tailed distribution in 2D. Perplexity controls the effective neighborhood size in the original space. Compare the local overlap for the same observation across settings. Spacing between separated groups, group areas, and plot orientation can change across fits. Use the original pixel-space distances to check claims about global separation.
