Many separators. One maximum margin.

Slides 11–20 · 4 minutes
● Class −1▲ Class +1Outer ring: fitted support vectorDashed lines: parallel supporting planes

Try a separating line first

f(x) = nᵀx − t; ‖n‖ = 1
M = minᵢ yᵢ f(xᵢ)

A separator requires M > 0. Moving the line changes the smallest signed distance. Maximizing that distance chooses the hard-margin classifier.

Ask before clickingWill moving a distant point farther away change the fitted boundary? What if a support point moves toward the gap?
Teaching sequence & worked answer
  1. Move the sliders: several lines classify every point correctly, but their margins differ.
  2. Show the maximum-margin fit. Initially f(x)=x₁: the boundary is x₁=0, the supporting planes are x₁=±1, and the full width is 2.
  3. Move the non-support point from (2,1.7) to (3,1.7): the optimum is unchanged.
  4. Move the support point from (1,−1) to (0.3,−1): the fitted boundary changes.

For canonical SVM coefficients, yᵢ(wᵀxᵢ+b)≥1; the distance to the boundary is |f(x)|/‖w‖. Thus each side has width 1/‖w‖ and the full band has width 2/‖w‖.

Support rings show nonzero dual coefficients from the fitted solution. In a degenerate dataset, some points on a supporting plane may have zero dual coefficient.

A correct prediction can still have a loss.

Click a point, or select it below, to inspect its prediction and hinge loss.

Cost of a margin violation

min ½‖w‖² + C Σᵢ ξᵢ
ξᵢ = max(0, 1 − yᵢf(xᵢ))

Here C is the penalty used by sklearn. A larger C puts more weight on reducing the total hinge loss. It does not guarantee fewer classification errors in every dataset.

Teaching sequence & the two meanings of C

Compare C=0.01 and C=100, then remove the contaminated observation. Inspect the width, support vectors and sum of losses.

Slide budget B: Σξᵢ≤B. A larger B allows more violations.
sklearn penalty C: CΣξᵢ appears in the objective. A larger C penalizes violations more strongly. They are different parameters; there is no universal numerical conversion such as C=1/B.

The displayed width is 2/‖w‖. The soft-margin band can contain observations from either class. Hinge loss is measured in score units, not directly in geometric distance.

A curved boundary can be linear somewhere else.

Expanded features (x, x²)

● Class −1: outer region▲ Class +1: middle regionHollow diamond: query
Ask studentsCan one threshold separate “outer–middle–outer” on the original line? How many thresholds appear when x²=1 is mapped back?

Worked example: two thresholds

Use x={−2,−1.5,−0.5,0,0.5,1.5,2}. Assign +1 to the middle three and −1 to the outer four.

φ(x) = (x, x²)
f(x) = 1 − x²
f(x)=0 ⇔ x=−1 or x=1

The line x²=1 in the expanded space becomes two boundaries in the original space. This is a constructed separating rule, rather than an optimized fit.

The kernel trick, with numbers

For a degree-2 polynomial kernel, use a different explicit map: ψ(x)=(1, √2x, x²).

Worked answer & teaching point

At x=0.5 and z=2, ψ(x)=(1,0.7071,0.25) and ψ(z)=(1,2.8284,4). Their inner product is 1+2+1=4, exactly (1+0.5×2)².

The √2 factor is necessary: (1+xz)²=1+2xz+x²z². The map (x,x²) alone instead gives the kernel xz+x²z².

Kernel substitution changes how inner products are computed; it does not require explicitly constructing the expanded features.

Two different controls

K(x,z) = exp(−γ‖x−z‖²)

Larger γ makes similarity more local. Larger C increases the penalty on training margin violations. Their effects must be considered together.

  3. Fit training noise: C=100, γ=30.
  4. Show test observations. Compare the same fitted decision rule on new observations.

Instructor notes · notation, corrections & implementation

Suggested order: 01 → 02 → 03 → 04, about 18 minutes. Slide references use the slide numbers printed in the supplied lecture, rather than PDF page indices.

  • Slides 20/30: in canonical scaling, a support plane is one score unit away, but its geometric distance is 1/‖w‖. Zero hinge loss requires y f(x)≥1, not merely correct classification.
  • Slides 25–28: distinguish a violation budget B from sklearn's penalty C.
  • Appendix slide 62: an inner product is not generally cosine similarity; cosine also divides by both feature-vector norms.

Linear and RBF fits are precomputed at the displayed discrete settings using sklearn SVC. Sliders select stored fits; no optimization runs in your browser. The geometric separator and feature-mapping query are calculated directly.
